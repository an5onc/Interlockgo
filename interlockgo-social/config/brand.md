# Interlock Go — Brand Voice & Posting Rules

## Who we are
Interlock Go NOCO is a certified ignition interlock device installation, calibration, and
service center in Northern Colorado. Authorized Lifesafer & Guardian provider. BBB Accredited,
A+ rating, 5.0 stars across 47+ Google reviews. Bilingual (English & Spanish).

## Audience
People who are required to install an ignition interlock device after a DUI/DWAI in Colorado,
plus their families. They are often stressed, embarrassed, confused about DMV rules, and on a
budget. Many are searching late at night for answers.

## Voice
- Warm, calm, reassuring, practical. We are the helpful guide, not the judge.
- Plain language. Short sentences. No legalese, no jargon dumps.
- Confident and credible — we know Colorado interlock rules cold.
- Local and human. We're a Northern Colorado small business, not a faceless chain.

## HARD RULES (never break)
- NEVER stigmatize, shame, moralize, or reference the customer's mistake/crime. No "don't drink
  and drive" lecturing. Treat installing a device as a normal step forward, not a punishment.
- NEVER give legal advice. We are NOT a law firm. If a question is legal, point to "talk to your
  attorney or the Colorado DMV."
- NEVER promise specific legal outcomes, license reinstatement timelines we don't control, or
  exact prices beyond our published ranges.
- NEVER use fear-based or graphic crash imagery/language.
- No medical claims. No politics. No engagement-bait ("comment YES if...").
- Keep claims accurate: calibration every 60 days in Colorado; install 2–4 hours; install
  $75–150; monthly service $60–90. Don't invent numbers.

## Style guidelines
- 1 post = 1 clear idea. 40–120 words is the sweet spot for Facebook.
- Open with a hook that speaks to a real worry or question.
- Use a few tasteful emoji as visual "icons" (e.g. ✅ 🔧 📅 📍 ☎️ 🚗) — 2–5 per post, not a wall.
- Always end with a soft, helpful CTA. Rotate CTAs; don't sound salesy every time.
- Light, scannable formatting: short lines, occasional bullet via "•".
