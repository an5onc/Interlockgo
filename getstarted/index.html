Get Started: Step‑by‑Step Guide

Everything you need to complete your ignition interlock requirements and finish the DMV reinstatement process — explained clearly.

Timelines and fees vary by case. When in doubt, call us — we'll walk you through it. 970‑515‑5740

1 Confirm your eligibility & hold requirements DMV / Court

Check your reinstatement letter for mandatory items — interlock period, SR‑22, alcohol/therapy classes, and reinstatement fees. If you're unsure, contact the Colorado DMV at 303‑205‑5600.

  • Schedule a DMV appointment to take your written exam — book early due to long wait times.
  • Schedule your installation at least 72 hours before your DMV exam. It takes 1–3 business days for the DMV to receive your lease agreement from us.

We can help you understand your letter and next steps. Call 970‑515‑5740 or email us.

2 Choose your ignition interlock & schedule install InterlockGo

We install Lifesafer and Guardian devices — both nationally approved and meeting all Colorado state requirements. Most installs take 2–3 hours.

  • Call us to schedule your installation.
  • Print and complete the client intake form before you arrive.
  • Bring: valid ID, vehicle registration, completed intake form, and a payment method. We accept cash, credit, and debit — no American Express.
  • Instructional videos are on our Lifesafer and Guardian pages. A one-on-one training session is provided before you leave the shop.
3 File insurance (SR‑22) with the DMV Insurance

Ask your insurer to submit an SR‑22 to the Colorado DMV electronically. Keep it active for the full required period — a lapse will trigger a license suspension.

  • Request proof of filing for your records.
  • Your insurance company will send proof of insurance directly to the DMV.
4 Complete alcohol education / treatment (if required) Classes

If your letter lists classes or therapy, enroll right away. The DMV requires a certificate of enrollment or completion before issuing a restricted permit.

  • Ask the provider for electronic submission to speed things up.
  • Proof of enrollment typically requires at least one class session completed.
5 Install day Shop Visit

Drive to the rear of our building — that's the garage entrance. Installation takes 2–3 hours depending on your vehicle's year, make, and model.

  • Training after installation takes about 15 minutes.
  • If you need to fill out your ignition interlock affidavit, we can assist you — notary services are available on-site.
  • Bring your affidavit and any other required documents to the DMV after installation.
  • We electronically submit your lease agreement to the DMV within 1–3 business days.
  • Depending on your program, you may be responsible for the installation labor fee and applicable taxes.
  • We accept cash, credit, and debit. No American Express.
6 Apply for your interlock‑restricted license DMV

Once your device is installed, SR‑22 is on file, and you're enrolled or completed with your alcohol classes, visit the DMV to take your written exam and receive your restricted permit.

  • Bring your ID and any fees listed on your reinstatement letter.
  • Confirm the length of your interlock requirement at this appointment.
  • Important: Your interlock program clock starts the day your restricted permit is issued — not the day of installation.
7 Return for regular calibrations & data uploads Ongoing

Stay on schedule to avoid lockouts and DMV violations. We assist with rescheduling, send reminders, and handle all reports required by the DMV.

  • Most clients calibrate monthly unless otherwise required.
  • Colorado clients are able to extend their daily interval between calibration appointments to 45 days.
  • Missing your calibration window will lock your device — don't let it lapse.
8 Removal & full reinstatement Finish

When your interlock period ends and the DMV issues your unrestricted license, you can schedule a removal appointment.

  • We monitor your compliance and will help guide you through the removal process when that time comes.
  • Do not attempt to remove the device yourself — always schedule with us.
  • Have questions about removal? Visit our FAQ page.

Quick FAQ

How long will I need interlock?

It varies by case. Unless eligible for early reinstatement, the average is 2 years. First-time DUI with BAC under .17 may qualify for 8 months with early reinstatement after 30 days.

What is an SR‑22?

It's proof of financial responsibility filed by your insurer with the DMV. It's not insurance itself — keep your policy active the entire time or face suspension.

What do I bring to the DMV for my first visit?

Bring your affidavit of enrollment or completion certificate, your ignition interlock affidavit, and your reinstatement application.
