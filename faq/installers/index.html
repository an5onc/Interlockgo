Finding an installer.

Short, plain answers about where to get a LifeSafer or Guardian interlock installed in Northern Colorado.

01Does the Colorado DMV give me a list of interlock shops near me?

No. The Colorado DMV's list of Colorado-approved interlock vendors names the four companies that make and supply the devices: Smart Start, LifeSafer, Intoxalock, and Guardian. It does not list the local shops that install them.

InterlockGo NOCO in Evans is not one of those four vendors. We are an authorized LifeSafer and Guardian installer. Call 970-515-5740.

02Who installs LifeSafer and Guardian interlocks in Northern Colorado?

InterlockGo NOCO in Evans. We are an authorized LifeSafer and Guardian installer: installation, calibration, scheduled service, and removal by a certified technician. Our Evans shop is listed on both the LifeSafer and Guardian location finders.

03Which interlock brands does InterlockGo install?

LifeSafer and Guardian. If your device is from Smart Start or Intoxalock, contact that provider to find their installer.

Not sure which device you need yet? Call 970-515-5740 and we'll confirm your program and match you to the right device.

Yes. The Colorado affidavit is notarized during the install appointment. No second stop.
